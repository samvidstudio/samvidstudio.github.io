Privacy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Samvid is a small studio in Dehradun, run by Amit Ghildiyal. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to the details you share with us.

What we collect

Only what you type into our enquiry form or send us on WhatsApp or email: your name, business name, business type, WhatsApp number, city, and, if you choose, your email address and a message.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, prepare a sample or quote for your business, and follow up about that enquiry. We do not use it for anything else.

Where it is kept

Your details are stored in our private Google Sheet and Google Workspace email, which only Samvid can access.

Who we share it with

No one. We never sell, rent or share your details with anyone else.

How long we keep it

For as long as we are in touch about your enquiry or project. If you would like us to delete your details, just ask and we will remove them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your details, or to stop contacting you. Write to amit@samvidstudio.com or WhatsApp +91 81939 68270. Reply "No thanks" to any message and we will not contact you again.

This website

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking for ads. If we add a visitor counter in future, it will count visits without identifying you, and we will update this page.
