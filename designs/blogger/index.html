Seven days, one rucksack, and the slowest road to Chopta

Why taking the long way through small villages beat every viewpoint on the map.

By [Author name]9 minute read

Morning over the ridge. Your own photo goes here.

The bus from Rishikesh left forty minutes late, which turned out to be the most useful thing that happened all week. It meant the driver stopped for tea in a village I had never heard of, and the lady at the stall told me about a trail her grandfather used.

That trail did not appear on any map. It took two days longer than the road. It was also the only part of the trip I still think about every morning.

This is a story about saying yes to slow detours, sleeping in homestays, and eating whatever the family is eating.
